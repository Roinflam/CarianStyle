### 📖 About

**CarianStyle** is an enchantment mod for Minecraft 1.12.2, inspired by the legendary weapons, sorceries, and abilities from **Elden Ring**. With over **100 unique enchantments**, this mod brings the strategic combat and risk-reward gameplay of the Lands Between into Minecraft.

### ✨ Features

- 🗡️ **100+ Unique Enchantments** - Combat skills, legendary sorceries, and defensive abilities
- ⚔️ **Ashes of War System** - Powerful weapon skills like Stamp Sweep, Repeating Thrust, and Cragblade
- 🔮 **Legendary Sorceries** - Devastating magical enchantments including Frenzied Burst and Aeonia
- 📿 **Special Enchantments** - Unique effects like Law of Causality and Corpse Piler
- 📊 **Percentage-Based Scaling** - Perfect compatibility with vanilla and modded weapons
- 🎮 **Vanilla-Based System** - No complex mechanics to learn, uses Minecraft's original enchanting system
- 🔧 **Highly Compatible** - Works seamlessly with weapon mods, even those dealing thousands of damage
- ⚖️ **Balanced Gameplay** - Risk-reward mechanics inspired by Elden Ring's design philosophy

### 📥 Installation

1. **Download Minecraft Forge** for 1.12.2 (latest version recommended)
   - [Download Forge →](https://files.minecraftforge.net/net/minecraftforge/forge/index_1.12.2.html)

2. **Download CarianStyle** from CurseForge

3. **Install the mod**
   - Place the `.jar` file into `.minecraft/mods/` folder
   - Launch Minecraft with Forge profile

4. **Done!** Enjoy over 100 new enchantments

### 💡 Why Percentage-Based Damage?

Traditional mods add **flat damage values** (e.g., +5 damage), which causes balance issues:
- ❌ Too weak with high-damage modded weapons
- ❌ Too strong with vanilla weapons
- ❌ Breaks balance across different mods

CarianStyle uses **percentage scaling** (e.g., +15% damage):
- ✅ Scales perfectly with any weapon
- ✅ Balanced across vanilla and modded content
- ✅ Works with weapons dealing 10 damage or 10,000 damage
- ✅ Maintains game balance

### 💡 为什么使用百分比伤害？

传统模组添加**固定伤害值**（如 +5 伤害），这会导致平衡问题：
- ❌ 对高伤害模组武器太弱
- ❌ 对原版武器太强
- ❌ 破坏不同模组间的平衡

卡利亚式使用**百分比缩放**（如 +15% 伤害）：
- ✅ 与任何武器完美缩放
- ✅ 在原版和模组内容间保持平衡
- ✅ 适用于造成 10 点伤害或 10,000 点伤害的武器
- ✅ 维持游戏平衡
