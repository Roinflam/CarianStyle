## 🌍 English

### 🌟 Features

- ✨ **100+ Unique Enchantments** - Inspired by Elden Ring's legendary equipment and abilities
- ⚔️ **Combat Skills (Ashes of War)** - Powerful weapon skills like Stamp Sweep, Repeating Thrust, and more
- 🔮 **Legendary Sorceries** - Devastating magical enchantments from the Lands Between
- 🛡️ **Defensive Enchantments** - Strategic protection and damage reduction abilities
- 🎯 **Percentage-Based Scaling** - All damage values scale with percentages, perfectly compatible with modded weapons
- 🎮 **Vanilla-Based System** - Built on Minecraft's original enchanting system, no complex mechanics to learn
- 🔧 **Highly Compatible** - Works seamlessly with other mods and modded weapons
- ⚖️ **Balanced Gameplay** - Risk-reward mechanics inspired by Elden Ring's design philosophy

### 📥 Installation

1. Download and install **Minecraft Forge** (Latest recommended version for 1.12.2)
    - [Download Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net/net/minecraftforge/forge/index_1.12.2.html)

2. Download **CarianStyle** from CurseForge
    - [Download CarianStyle](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/carianstyle)

3. Place the `.jar` file into your `mods` folder
    - Located in `.minecraft/mods/`

4. Launch Minecraft with Forge profile and enjoy!

### 💡 Why Percentage-Based?

Unlike traditional mods that add flat damage values, CarianStyle uses **percentage scaling** for all damage calculations. This means:

✅ Works perfectly with vanilla weapons  
✅ Scales naturally with modded weapons (even those dealing thousands of damage)  
✅ Maintains balance across different weapon types  
✅ No "too weak" or "too strong" issues with other mods

### 💡 为什么使用百分比伤害？

与传统模组添加固定伤害值不同，CarianStyle 对所有伤害计算使用**百分比缩放**。这意味着：

✅ 与原版武器完美配合  
✅ 与模组武器自然缩放（即使是造成数千伤害的武器）  
✅ 在不同武器类型间保持平衡  
✅ 与其他模组不会出现"太弱"或"太强"的问题

## ❓ FAQ / 常见问题

<details>
<summary><b>Q: 这个模组与其他武器模组兼容吗？</b></summary>

**A:** 完全兼容！由于所有伤害都是百分比计算，无论武器基础伤害多少，附魔效果都会按比例缩放。

**Q: Is this mod compatible with other weapon mods?**

**A:** Absolutely! Since all damage is percentage-based, enchantment effects scale proportionally regardless of weapon base damage.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Q: 我需要安装其他模组吗？</b></summary>

**A:** 不需要！但强烈推荐安装 [Enchantment Descriptions](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enchantment-descriptions) 来查看附魔说明。

**Q: Do I need to install other mods?**

**A:** No! But we highly recommend [Enchantment Descriptions](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enchantment-descriptions) to see enchantment tooltips.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Q: 附魔太多了，怎么记住它们？</b></summary>

**A:** 安装 Enchantment Descriptions 后，将鼠标悬停在附魔书或附魔物品上即可看到详细说明。同时可以查阅[百科](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/6247.html)。

**Q: There are so many enchantments, how do I remember them?**

**A:** Install Enchantment Descriptions and hover over enchanted books/items to see descriptions. Also check the [Wiki](https://www.mcmod.cn/class/6247.html).

</details>

**Q: Can I use this on a server?**
